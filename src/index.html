How performance and plans are calculated

Target = the month's volume plan (dealer-wise plan in the data pack, summed over the dealers of the SE, ASM or zone). Achievement = net units billed month to date (Cycle_Billing). Ach % = achievement ÷ target. ASM and zone percentages are always Σ achievement ÷ Σ target, never an average of SE percentages.

Vs pace = achievement ÷ (target × working days gone ÷ working days in the month). Working days exclude Sundays only (no holiday calendar in the data). Daily target = remaining gap ÷ working days left. Weekly target = plan × working days in the week ÷ working days in the month. Weeks follow the SE Evaluation workbook: W1 1–7, W2 8–14, W3 15–21, W4 22–28, W5 29–month end.

Status from vs pace: On track, Watch, Behind, Critical (thresholds in Settings below; defaults 100%, 90%, 70%). No plan = No target. Priority of an action: Critical/Behind → High, Watch/No target → Medium, On track → Low. Trend compares the last two complete weeks: Improving / Declining beyond the stable band, Strong & consistent when both are on track, Consistently low when every complete week is below Watch, otherwise Stable; fewer than two complete weeks = Insufficient data.

Action plans are rule-based: each gap in the data (volume pace, billing points, NPD, range, premium, overdue, journey plan, a fall in the last 7 days, open orders, carried-forward actions) becomes an action with its evidence, owner, deadline, expected outcome and follow-up. Where the data shows a gap but not its cause, the action is marked Cause needs investigation. Dealer names come from the dealer master only.

DJP / journey plan: planned vs made visits come from the Journey Plan Update in the data pack (currently W1–W2 only); it is not refreshed by the daily dump.

Data & security

This Phase 1 site is static. It contains no company data: the data pack and daily dump are read and stored only in the browser that loads them. Anyone with the site link can open the empty dashboard, but sees nothing until they load a data file themselves.

Action status and remarks are saved in this browser only. They are not shared with other users or devices. Use Action tracking → Backup to move them. Clearing browser data deletes them.

The planned Zonal and Admin logins (Phase 2) need a backend with real authentication and server-side permissions; hiding parts of this page would not secure anything, so no login screen is included yet.

Publishing an update (admin): upload the new master workbook here, then click Publish update. It encrypts the data with the team password and downloads data.js. Put it in the js folder of the Netlify upload folder (replacing the old one) and drag the folder to Netlify again. Everyone sees the new data on their next visit.

Data Management → Upload & Publish has the Publish update and Remove data buttons.
